1. What the app is

Know Your Block is an educational walking companion for New York City's honorary street co-namings: streets officially co-named by the NYC Council to honor notable people. The app presents each honoree's story and lets you record having visited their street in person.

The app is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the City of New York, the NYC Council, the Department of City Planning, Mapbox, OpenStreetMap, or any honoree, honoree's family, or estate.
