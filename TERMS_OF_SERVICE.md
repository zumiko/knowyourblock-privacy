## 1. What the app is

Know Your Block is an educational walking companion for New York City's honorary street
co-namings: streets officially co-named by the NYC Council to honor notable people. The app
presents each honoree's story and lets you record having visited their street in person.

## 3. Accuracy of content

The app's stories and street locations are curated from public records, including NYC
Council co-naming records and the NYC Department of City Planning's street-name GIS data.
